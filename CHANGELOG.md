# Changelog

## v1.0.0

First release.

- Drag and drop for every Titan plugin, onto any place on any Titan bar, with a line showing where it will land.
- A centre area on every bar, besides its left and right ends.
- Plugins are locked unless you are arranging. Arranging starts with Edit Mode, the Arranger button on the bar,
  `/arranger` or a key binding, and stops in combat. Bars set to auto-hide stay out while you arrange.
- While arranging: a panel listing the plugins on no bar under Titan's own groups (drag them on to show them, drag a
  plugin onto it to hide it), a spacing slider for each area (left, centre, right), a click on a plugin for its side,
  display options and its margins on either side (below zero too), Short bars that move and size without modifier
  keys, arrow keys to move a plugin or a Short bar, and Revert.
- The panel and the options popup are built like Edit Mode's windows, with a Titan bar in your skin or colour for a
  title, and Titan's font. The panel is as tall as its list (up to 85% of the screen, stopping above Titan's
  bottom bars), can be moved and made wider or shorter, and opens beside Edit Mode's window when you arrange from
  Edit Mode.
