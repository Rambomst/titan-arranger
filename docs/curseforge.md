## Summary

Drag and drop arranging for Titan Panel, with a lock and Edit Mode support.

## Description

Titan Panel places its plugins through right-click menus: a checkbox for the side, "take it off and put it back"
for the order, and a separate Short bar for anything in the middle. **Titan Panel [Arranger]** lets you put every
plugin exactly where you want it by dragging, and keeps it there.

- **Drag and drop:** drag any plugin to any place on any Titan bar. A gold line shows exactly where it will land.
- **Left, centre or right:** every bar has a centre area as well as its two ends.
- **Locked unless you are arranging:** start arranging by opening Edit Mode, clicking the Arranger button on your
  bar, with /arranger or with a key binding. Otherwise plugins stay put.
- **Show and hide by dragging:** while arranging, a panel lists the plugins on no bar. Drag one onto a bar to show
  it, or drag a plugin onto the panel to hide it.
- **Plugin options in one click:** click a plugin while arranging for its side, display choices and margins.
- **Short bars without modifier keys:** drag to move, drag the right edge to size.
- **Spacing for each area:** left, centre and right, changing as you slide, plus margins for any single plugin,
  below zero to pull it closer to its neighbour.
- **Fits in:** the panel and popup look like Edit Mode's windows, titled with a Titan bar in your skin or colour.
- **Arrow keys** move a selected plugin one place, or a selected Short bar one pixel.
- **Revert** undoes everything since arranging started.

Works with every plugin Titan shows: Titan's own, third-party Titan plugins and LibDataBroker plugins. Your layout
stays in Titan's own settings, per profile. Needs Titan Panel 9.3.7 or later.
